VIBE stands for Ventures in Business Engineering. We are an Opus-supported experiential learning initiative, led by faculty and staff with student leadership at its core.

We bring business knowledge, data, design, and AI-assisted coding together. Students define a problem, build a working solution, test its assumptions, and explain what it can—and cannot—do.

How the lab works

Guided by experience.
Driven by students.

VIBE brings faculty and staff guidance, student leadership, collaborative project teams, and an advisory board together to support useful, hands-on work.

01

Faculty & staff leadership

Guide the lab's direction, mentor students, and help coordinate projects and resources.

02

Student leadership

Organize activities, connect students with opportunities, and support collaboration across project teams.

03

Project teams

Bring different academic backgrounds together to define problems, build useful tools, and share their work.

04

Board of Advisors

Made up of staff, faculty, and professionals, the board brings perspectives from campus and industry to support the lab's work.
