- Use **VIBE Lab**, rather than VIBE Club. Present VIBE as an Opus-supported faculty/staff-led experiential learning initiative with student leadership, consistent with the current proposal and the user's correction.
- Describe the linked learning tools as faculty-created. Keep student project showcases separate from lab project directions; do not claim all linked student work was commissioned or completed by VIBE Lab.
- Present shared projects as **example briefs**. No unverified completed project names, partner organizations, or outcomes are invented.
- The roadmap and May 2028 counts are **planning targets**, not accomplishments or evidence of a fellowship award. The site does not announce an Innovation Fellows award.
- High school pilots are planned, not described as completed partnerships. The existing tools are already available; the roadmap therefore refers to expanding the collection rather than waiting until 2027 to launch it.
- Use workshop dates without exact times or room numbers, because later organizer communications can supersede the poster's logistics. Direct event questions to its named organizer.

- Financial disclaimers appear next to learning tools, next to showcases, and in the footer. The decorative Market Lab chart is explicitly an illustration, not actual market data.
